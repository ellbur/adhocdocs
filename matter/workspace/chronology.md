# Chronology: PO-BMS-26-0112 (Harrowgate / Bellwether)

The shipment, from the purchase order on 9 February 2026 through the carrier's return of the pallet on 4 March, Harrowgate's demand on 25 March and Bellwether's response on 2 April. A short note on what followed is at the end. Each entry gives the date, what happened, and what the people involved wrote at the time. Each document is cited by its file name.

**How to read this.** Quotations are the documents' own words, checked against the text. Anything marked *Inference* is my reading, not something a document says. Three key documents are scans with no text layer: the carrier's delivery receipt (`pod-TCF-889104.pdf`), Bellwether's receiving discrepancy report (`receiving-discrepancy-report-RDR-0224-02.pdf`) and the carrier's claim letter (`tcfl-claim-letter-2026-04-10.pdf`). I read them by eye from the page images, and they are shown as my readings, not as quotations.

**What was left out.** The privilege screen is set to *suspected*. It holds back Bellwether's internal emails with its General Counsel about the demand (26–27 March), the litigation-hold notice and a reply to it, and a memo marked privileged (`privileged-memo-harrowgate-2026-03-27.docx`). I did not read them, and nothing below relies on them.

## The tip: where the two sides part

1. **Was the shipment delivered?** The carrier's receipt and scan say it was delivered to Dock 4 on 24 Feb at 2:47 pm, signed "TK", with no exceptions. Bellwether says nothing was received *against PO-BMS-26-0112*. Its own records show that one unlabeled pallet in HFS-printed wrap came off that truck and sat in quarantine bay Q-3 until 4 March. (24 Feb, 10 Mar, 2 Apr.)
2. **Who wrote "TK"?** Bellwether's response says Thomas Kettering did not sign. Bellwether's own discrepancy report, dock log and camera notes all say dock associate José Ibáñez signed the driver's handheld and wrote "TK". The 2 April response does not mention this. (24 Feb, 2 Apr.)
3. **Was the PO or packing list on the pallet?** Bellwether's dock records say there was no PO and no packing list. Harrowgate's account manager says the warehouse told her the packing-list sleeve was on the pallet when it left Columbus. (24 Feb, 3 Apr.)
4. **What was said on the 2 March call?** Zoë Müller says she told Dana Okafor the order hadn't arrived. Okafor's notes say Müller only asked for an ETA. 2 March was also the last day of the MSA's ten-day notice window, and that clause requires notice *in writing*. (2 Mar, 25 Mar, 2 Apr.)
5. **Did anyone connect the Q-3 pallet to the order before it went back?** On 2 March Müller was chasing the order and Kettering was sending away an HFS pallet. Neither connected the two until 10 March. (2 Mar, 10 Mar.)
