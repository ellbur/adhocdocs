# Time limits in the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure and Title 9 (Arbitration)

**Sources:** `uscourts-federal-rules-of-civil-procedure.pdf` (FRCP as amended to Dec. 1, 2025, with the Supplemental Rules) and `usc-title9-arbitration-2023.pdf` (9 U.S.C.).

**How to read an entry:** each one gives the rule, how long the period is, what starts the clock, what it applies to, and who it binds. Those four fields are my summaries. The block quotation under each entry is the source text itself; it is a checked reference to the document. Where a field is my inference rather than the rule's words, it is marked "inference". Many periods give way to a stipulation, local rule, court order, or statute, as the quoted language shows.

**Scope and method:** I read Rules 1–87, the Supplemental Rules (A–G and the Social Security rules), and Title 9 chapters 1–4. I swept every line of the operative text for "day", "week", "month", "year", and "hour" (singular and plural) and read the passages around each hit. This list covers **stated periods** only. It leaves out (a) limits with no stated length ("promptly", "reasonable time", "before trial", "at the time of"), (b) the Appendix of Forms, whose Rule 4 waiver forms repeat the Rule 4(d) periods, (c) editorial and statutory notes, and (d) age and fee rules such as the 18-year-old server requirement and one day's attendance fee in Rule 45(b)(1), which aren't deadlines.

## 1. How periods are counted (FRCP 6)

These rules don't set deadlines of their own. They control how every period below is counted.

### FRCP 6(a)(1)

- **Period:** Periods stated in days or longer
- **Clock starts:** The event that triggers the period (that day is excluded)
- **Applies to:** Any period in the FRCP, a local rule, a court order, or a statute that doesn't say how to count
- **Binds:** Everyone counting a period

::q{#r6a1}

### FRCP 6(a)(2)

- **Period:** Periods stated in hours
- **Clock starts:** The triggering event itself (counting starts at once)
- **Applies to:** Hour-based periods
- **Binds:** Everyone counting a period

::q{#r6a2}

### FRCP 6(a)(4)

- **Period:** The last day ends at midnight (e-filing) or when the clerk's office closes (other filing)
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** The last day of any period, unless a statute, local rule, or order says otherwise
- **Binds:** Filers

::q{#r6a4}

### FRCP 6(b)(2)

- **Period:** No extensions
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Motions under Rules 50(b) and (d), 52(b), 59(b), (d), and (e), and 60(b)
- **Binds:** The court

::q{#r6b2}

### FRCP 6(d)

- **Period:** 3 days added
- **Clock starts:** Service by mail, by leaving with the clerk, or by other consented means (Rule 5(b)(2)(C), (D), (F))
- **Applies to:** Any period that runs from being served
- **Binds:** The party who must act

::q{#r6d}

## 2. Starting the case and service

### FRCP 4(d)(1)(F)

- **Period:** At least 30 days (at least 60 if the defendant is outside any U.S. judicial district)
- **Clock starts:** The waiver request is sent
- **Applies to:** Time a waiver request must give the defendant to return the waiver
- **Binds:** Plaintiff (sets the date); defendant (returns by it)

::q{#r4d1f}

### FRCP 4(d)(3)

- **Period:** 60 days (90 if sent outside any U.S. judicial district)
- **Clock starts:** The waiver request is sent
- **Applies to:** Answer after a timely waiver
- **Binds:** Defendant who waives

::q{#r4d3}

### FRCP 4(m)

- **Period:** 90 days
- **Clock starts:** The complaint is filed
- **Applies to:** Serving the summons and complaint (dismissal without prejudice unless good cause is shown)
- **Binds:** Plaintiff (Rule 4(c)(1) makes the plaintiff responsible for timely service)

::q{#r4m}

### FRCP 5.1(c)

- **Period:** 60 days, unless the court sets a later time
- **Clock starts:** The notice is filed or the court certifies the challenge, whichever is earlier
- **Applies to:** Intervention by the attorney general in a constitutional challenge to a statute
- **Binds:** Attorney general; the court (no final judgment holding the statute unconstitutional before the time expires)

::q{#r51c}

## 3. Pleadings and responses

### FRCP 12(a)(1)(A)

- **Period:** 21 days; or 60 days (90 outside the U.S.) after a waiver
- **Clock starts:** Service of the summons and complaint; or the waiver request is sent
- **Applies to:** Answer to the complaint
- **Binds:** Defendant
- **Note:** Rule 12(a) gives way to a different time set by a federal statute.

::q{#r12a1a}

### FRCP 12(a)(1)(B)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the pleading stating the counterclaim or crossclaim
- **Applies to:** Answer to a counterclaim or crossclaim
- **Binds:** The party it's asserted against

::q{#r12a1b}

### FRCP 12(a)(1)(C)

- **Period:** 21 days, unless the order sets a different time
- **Clock starts:** Service of an order to reply
- **Applies to:** Reply to an answer
- **Binds:** The party ordered to reply

::q{#r12a1c}

### FRCP 12(a)(2)

- **Period:** 60 days
- **Clock starts:** Service on the United States attorney
- **Applies to:** Answer by the United States, an agency, or an officer or employee sued in an official capacity
- **Binds:** U.S. government defendants

::q{#r12a2}

### FRCP 12(a)(3)

- **Period:** 60 days
- **Clock starts:** Service on the officer or employee, or on the U.S. attorney, whichever is later
- **Applies to:** Answer by a U.S. officer or employee sued individually for acts in connection with federal duties
- **Binds:** Individual-capacity federal defendants

::q{#r12a3}

### FRCP 12(a)(4)

- **Period:** 14 days, unless the court sets a different time
- **Clock starts:** Notice that a Rule 12 motion was denied or postponed; or service of a more definite statement
- **Applies to:** Responsive pleading after a Rule 12 motion
- **Binds:** The responding party

::q{#r12a4}

### FRCP 12(e)

- **Period:** 14 days, or the time the court sets
- **Clock starts:** Notice of an order for a more definite statement
- **Applies to:** Complying with the order (or the pleading may be struck)
- **Binds:** The pleader

::q{#r12e}

### FRCP 12(f)(2)

- **Period:** 21 days (when no response is allowed)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the pleading
- **Applies to:** Motion to strike
- **Binds:** Moving party

::q{#r12f2}

### FRCP 11(c)(2)

- **Period:** 21 days, or another time the court sets
- **Clock starts:** Service of the sanctions motion
- **Applies to:** Safe harbor: the motion can't be filed if the challenged paper is withdrawn or corrected within this time
- **Binds:** Movant (must wait); opposing party (may cure)

::q{#r11c2}

### FRCP 14(a)(1)

- **Period:** 14 days (after that, leave of court is needed)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the third-party plaintiff's original answer
- **Applies to:** Filing a third-party complaint without leave
- **Binds:** Defending party / third-party plaintiff

::q{#r14a1}

### FRCP 15(a)(1)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Serving the pleading; or, if a response is required, service of the responsive pleading or of a Rule 12(b), (e), or (f) motion, whichever is earlier
- **Applies to:** Amending once as a matter of course
- **Binds:** The pleading party

::q{#r15a1}

### FRCP 15(a)(3)

- **Period:** The time left to respond to the original pleading, or 14 days, whichever is later (unless the court orders otherwise)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the amended pleading
- **Applies to:** Response to an amended pleading
- **Binds:** The responding party

::q{#r15a3}

### FRCP 15(c)(1)(C)

- **Period:** The Rule 4(m) period (90 days)
- **Clock starts:** The complaint is filed (through Rule 4(m))
- **Applies to:** Notice to a party added by amendment, for the amendment to relate back
- **Binds:** Plaintiff (the new party must have had notice within this period)

::q{#r15c1c}

### FRCP 81(c)(2)

- **Period:** The longest of: 21 days after receiving the initial pleading; 21 days after service of the summons; or 7 days after the notice of removal is filed
- **Clock starts:** See period
- **Applies to:** Answer or other defenses in a removed action, if the defendant hadn't answered before removal
- **Binds:** Defendant in a removed action

::q{#r81c2}

## 4. Jury demand

### FRCP 38(b)(1)

- **Period:** 14 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the last pleading directed to the issue
- **Applies to:** Serving a jury demand
- **Binds:** Any party wanting a jury (otherwise waived under Rule 38(d))

::q{#r38b}

### FRCP 38(c)

- **Period:** 14 days, or a shorter time the court orders
- **Clock starts:** Service of a demand limited to some issues
- **Applies to:** Another party's demand on other or all issues
- **Binds:** Other parties

::q{#r38c}

### FRCP 81(c)(3)(B)

- **Period:** 14 days
- **Clock starts:** Filing a notice of removal, or being served with one filed by another party
- **Applies to:** Jury demand after removal, if all pleadings were served before removal
- **Binds:** Parties in removed actions

::q{#r81c3b}

## 5. Parties and class actions

### FRCP 23(f)

- **Period:** 14 days (45 if any party is the United States, a U.S. agency, or a U.S. officer or employee sued for acts on the United States' behalf)
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the order granting or denying class certification
- **Applies to:** Petition for permission to appeal, filed with the circuit clerk
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r23f}

### FRCP 25(a)(1)

- **Period:** 90 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of a statement noting the death
- **Applies to:** Motion to substitute for a deceased party (or the action must be dismissed)
- **Binds:** Parties and the decedent's successor or representative

::q{#r25a1}

## 6. Case management and the discovery conference

### FRCP 16(b)(2)

- **Period:** The earlier of 90 days after any defendant is served or 60 days after any defendant appears (unless there's good cause for delay)
- **Clock starts:** Service on, or appearance by, any defendant
- **Applies to:** Issuing the scheduling order
- **Binds:** The judge

::q{#r16b2}

### FRCP 26(f)(1)

- **Period:** At least 21 days before
- **Clock starts:** The scheduling conference, or the date a scheduling order is due under Rule 16(b)
- **Applies to:** The parties' Rule 26(f) conference
- **Binds:** The parties

::q{#r26f1}

### FRCP 26(f)(2)

- **Period:** 14 days
- **Clock starts:** The Rule 26(f) conference
- **Applies to:** Written report outlining the discovery plan
- **Binds:** Attorneys of record and unrepresented parties who have appeared (jointly)

::q{#r26f2}

### FRCP 26(f)(4)

- **Period:** A local rule may set less than 21 days (conference) and less than 14 days (report)
- **Clock starts:** As in 26(f)(1) and (2)
- **Applies to:** Courts with expedited Rule 16(b) schedules
- **Binds:** Courts (by local rule)

::q{#r26f4}

### FRCP 26(d)(2)

- **Period:** More than 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the summons and complaint on a party
- **Applies to:** Delivering early Rule 34 requests (treated as served at the first Rule 26(f) conference)
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r26d2}

## 7. Disclosures and discovery

### FRCP 26(a)(1)(C)

- **Period:** At or within 14 days, unless a stipulation or order sets a different time
- **Clock starts:** The Rule 26(f) conference
- **Applies to:** Initial disclosures
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r26a1c}

### FRCP 26(a)(1)(D)

- **Period:** 30 days, unless a stipulation or order sets a different time
- **Clock starts:** Being served or joined after the Rule 26(f) conference
- **Applies to:** Initial disclosures by parties joined later
- **Binds:** Late-joined parties

::q{#r26a1d}

### FRCP 26(a)(2)(B)(iv)–(v)

- **Period:** Look-back periods: 10 years (publications) and 4 years (prior testimony)
- **Clock starts:** Counted back from the report
- **Applies to:** Contents of a retained expert's written report
- **Binds:** The party disclosing the expert

::q{#r26a2b}

### FRCP 26(a)(2)(D)

- **Period:** At least 90 days before trial (or the ready-for-trial date); rebuttal within 30 days
- **Clock starts:** The trial date; for rebuttal, the other party's disclosure
- **Applies to:** Expert disclosures, absent a stipulation or order
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r26a2d}

### FRCP 27(a)(2)

- **Period:** At least 21 days before
- **Clock starts:** The hearing on the petition
- **Applies to:** Serving notice of a petition to perpetuate testimony
- **Binds:** Petitioner

::q{#r27a2}

### FRCP 30(d)(1)

- **Period:** 1 day of 7 hours, unless stipulated or ordered otherwise
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Length of a deposition
- **Binds:** Parties (the court must allow more time where Rule 30(d)(1) requires)

::q{#r30d1}

### FRCP 30(e)(1)

- **Period:** 30 days
- **Clock starts:** The officer's notice that the transcript or recording is available
- **Applies to:** The deponent's review, and signing a statement of changes (if requested before the deposition ends)
- **Binds:** Deponent

::q{#r30e1}

### FRCP 31(a)(5)

- **Period:** Cross-questions 14 days; redirect 7 days; recross 7 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the notice and direct questions; of cross-questions; of redirect questions
- **Applies to:** Questions for a deposition by written questions
- **Binds:** Parties (the court may extend or shorten for good cause)

::q{#r31a5}

### FRCP 32(a)(5)(A)

- **Period:** Less than 14 days' notice
- **Clock starts:** Notice of the deposition
- **Applies to:** A deposition can't be used against a party who got short notice and promptly moved for a protective order that was still pending
- **Binds:** Party offering the deposition

::q{#r32a5a}

### FRCP 32(d)(3)(C)

- **Period:** The time for serving responsive questions; for a recross-question, 7 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the question
- **Applies to:** Objection to the form of a written question (waived if late)
- **Binds:** Party objecting

::q{#r32d3c}

### FRCP 33(b)(2)

- **Period:** 30 days (may be stipulated under Rule 29 or ordered)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the interrogatories
- **Applies to:** Answers and objections to interrogatories
- **Binds:** Responding party

::q{#r33b2}

### FRCP 34(b)(2)(A)

- **Period:** 30 days; for early requests, 30 days after the first Rule 26(f) conference (may be stipulated or ordered)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the request, or the Rule 26(f) conference
- **Applies to:** Written response to requests for production
- **Binds:** Responding party

::q{#r34b2a}

### FRCP 36(a)(3)

- **Period:** 30 days (may be stipulated or ordered)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the request for admission
- **Applies to:** Answer or objection (matters are otherwise admitted)
- **Binds:** Responding party

::q{#r36a3}

### FRCP 45(d)(2)(B)

- **Period:** The earlier of the compliance date or 14 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the subpoena
- **Applies to:** Written objection to a subpoena for documents or inspection
- **Binds:** The person commanded to produce, or its designee

::q{#r45d2b}

## 8. Motions generally

### FRCP 6(c)(1)

- **Period:** At least 14 days before
- **Clock starts:** The time set for the hearing
- **Applies to:** Serving a written motion and notice of the hearing (except ex parte motions, or where the rules or a court order set a different time)
- **Binds:** The moving party

::q{#r6c1}

### FRCP 6(c)(2)

- **Period:** At least 7 days before (except as Rule 59(c) provides)
- **Clock starts:** The hearing
- **Applies to:** Serving an opposing affidavit (supporting affidavits are served with the motion)
- **Binds:** The opposing party

::q{#r6c2}

### FRCP 56(b)

- **Period:** Until 30 days after the close of all discovery (unless a local rule or order says otherwise)
- **Clock starts:** Close of all discovery
- **Applies to:** Summary judgment motion
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r56b}

### FRCP 53(f)(2)

- **Period:** 21 days, unless the court sets a different time
- **Clock starts:** Service of a copy of the master's order, report, or recommendations
- **Applies to:** Objections, or a motion to adopt or modify
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r53f2}

### FRCP 72(a)

- **Period:** 14 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of a copy of the magistrate judge's order
- **Applies to:** Objections to a nondispositive order (a defect not timely objected to can't be assigned as error)
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r72a}

### FRCP 72(b)(2)

- **Period:** 14 days to object; 14 days to respond
- **Clock starts:** Service of the recommended disposition; or of the objections
- **Applies to:** Objections to a magistrate judge's recommended disposition, and responses
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r72b2}

## 9. Temporary restraining orders

### FRCP 65(b)(2)

- **Period:** Not to exceed 14 days, extendable for a like period for good cause (or longer if the adverse party consents)
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the order
- **Applies to:** Duration of a TRO issued without notice
- **Binds:** The court

::q{#r65b2}

### FRCP 65(b)(4)

- **Period:** 2 days' notice, or shorter notice the court sets
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Motion to dissolve or modify a TRO issued without notice
- **Binds:** Adverse party (gives notice to the party who got the order)

::q{#r65b4}

## 10. Pretrial, trial, and offers of judgment

### FRCP 26(a)(3)(B)

- **Period:** Disclosures at least 30 days before trial; objections within 14 days after (unless the court orders or sets otherwise)
- **Clock starts:** Trial date; for objections, the disclosures being made
- **Applies to:** Pretrial disclosures and objections to them
- **Binds:** Parties

::q{#r26a3b}

### FRCP 68(a)

- **Period:** Offer at least 14 days before trial; acceptance within 14 days
- **Clock starts:** The trial date; for acceptance, service of the offer
- **Applies to:** Offer of judgment and its acceptance
- **Binds:** Defending party (offer); opposing party (acceptance)

::q{#r68a}

### FRCP 68(c)

- **Period:** At least 14 days before
- **Clock starts:** The hearing to determine the extent of liability
- **Applies to:** Offer of judgment after liability is decided
- **Binds:** The party held liable

::q{#r68c}

## 11. Judgment and after judgment

Rule 6(b)(2) bars extending the Rule 50(b) and (d), 52(b), 59(b), (d), and (e), and 60(b) periods (see section 1).

### FRCP 50(b)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of judgment; or, for a jury issue not decided by a verdict, discharge of the jury
- **Applies to:** Renewed motion for judgment as a matter of law
- **Binds:** Movant

::q{#r50b}

### FRCP 50(d)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the judgment
- **Applies to:** New-trial motion by the party against whom JMOL is rendered
- **Binds:** Losing party

::q{#r50d}

### FRCP 52(b)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of judgment
- **Applies to:** Motion to amend or add findings
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r52b}

### FRCP 54(d)(1)

- **Period:** Clerk taxes costs on 14 days' notice; motion for review within the next 7 days
- **Clock starts:** The clerk's notice; then the clerk's action
- **Applies to:** Taxing costs and review of the clerk's action
- **Binds:** Clerk; parties

::q{#r54d1}

### FRCP 54(d)(2)(B)(i)

- **Period:** 14 days, unless a statute or court order provides otherwise
- **Clock starts:** Entry of judgment
- **Applies to:** Motion for attorney's fees
- **Binds:** Party claiming fees

::q{#r54d2b}

### FRCP 55(b)(2)

- **Period:** At least 7 days before
- **Clock starts:** The hearing on the application
- **Applies to:** Written notice of a default-judgment application to a defaulting party who has appeared
- **Binds:** Party seeking default judgment

::q{#r55b2}

### FRCP 58(c)(2)

- **Period:** 150 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry in the civil docket
- **Applies to:** When a judgment that needs a separate document counts as entered if no separate document is issued (this drives the post-judgment and appeal clocks)
- **Binds:** Everyone counting from entry of judgment

::q{#r58c2}

### FRCP 59(b)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of judgment
- **Applies to:** Motion for a new trial
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r59b}

### FRCP 59(c)

- **Period:** 14 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the new-trial motion's affidavits
- **Applies to:** Opposing affidavits
- **Binds:** Opposing party

::q{#r59c}

### FRCP 59(d)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of judgment
- **Applies to:** New trial on the court's own initiative, or for a reason not in the motion
- **Binds:** The court

::q{#r59d}

### FRCP 59(e)

- **Period:** 28 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the judgment
- **Applies to:** Motion to alter or amend a judgment
- **Binds:** Any party

::q{#r59e}

### FRCP 60(c)(1)

- **Period:** Within a reasonable time; for reasons (1)–(3), no more than a year
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the judgment or order, or the date of the proceeding
- **Applies to:** Motion for relief from a judgment or order
- **Binds:** Movant

::q{#r60c1}

### FRCP 62(a)

- **Period:** 30 days, unless the court orders otherwise
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the judgment
- **Applies to:** Automatic stay of execution and enforcement
- **Binds:** Judgment creditor (can't execute); court

::q{#r62a}

## 12. Condemnation and civil-rules emergencies

### FRCP 71.1(d)(2)(A)(v)

- **Period:** 21 days (stated in the notice)
- **Clock starts:** Service of the notice
- **Applies to:** Content of the condemnation notice: the time to answer
- **Binds:** Plaintiff (must state it)

::q{#r711d2v}

### FRCP 71.1(d)(3)(B)

- **Period:** Once a week for at least 3 successive weeks
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Service of the condemnation notice by publication
- **Binds:** Plaintiff

::q{#r711d3b}

### FRCP 71.1(e)(2)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of the notice
- **Applies to:** Answer objecting to the taking
- **Binds:** Condemnation defendant

::q{#r711e2}

### FRCP 87(b)(1)(C)

- **Period:** No more than 90 days
- **Clock starts:** The declaration of a rules emergency
- **Applies to:** How long a declaration lasts
- **Binds:** Judicial Conference

::q{#r87b1c}

### FRCP 87(c)(2)(A)

- **Period:** Extension of no more than 30 days
- **Clock starts:** Entry of the court's order
- **Applies to:** During a declared emergency, extending the time for Rules 50(b) and (d), 52(b), 59(b), (d), and (e), and 60(b) motions
- **Binds:** The court

::q{#r87c2a}

## 13. Supplemental Rules: admiralty, maritime, and asset forfeiture

These apply only in admiralty or maritime and in rem forfeiture actions.

### Supp. Rule B(3)(a)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Service of process on the garnishee
- **Applies to:** Garnishee's answer, and answers to interrogatories served with the complaint
- **Binds:** Garnishee

::q{#rb3a}

### Supp. Rule B(3)(b)

- **Period:** 30 days
- **Clock starts:** Execution of process (attachment or service on the garnishee)
- **Applies to:** Defendant's answer
- **Binds:** Defendant

::q{#rb3b}

### Supp. Rule C(4)

- **Period:** Not released within 14 days, then public notice promptly or within the time the court allows
- **Clock starts:** Execution of process
- **Applies to:** Public notice of an in rem action and arrest
- **Binds:** Plaintiff

::q{#rc4}

### Supp. Rule C(6)(a)(i)

- **Period:** 14 days, or the time the court allows
- **Clock starts:** Execution of process
- **Applies to:** Verified statement of right or interest
- **Binds:** Anyone asserting possession or ownership

::q{#rc6ai}

### Supp. Rule C(6)(a)(iv)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Filing the statement of interest or right
- **Applies to:** Answer in an in rem action
- **Binds:** Claimant

::q{#rc6aiv}

### Supp. Rule F(1)

- **Period:** Not later than 6 months
- **Clock starts:** Receipt of a written claim
- **Applies to:** Vessel owner's complaint for limitation of liability
- **Binds:** Vessel owner

::q{#rf1}

### Supp. Rule F(4)

- **Period:** Claim date at least 30 days after the notice issues; publication once a week for 4 successive weeks before it; mailing by the day of second publication
- **Clock starts:** Issuance of the notice
- **Applies to:** Notice to claimants in a limitation proceeding
- **Binds:** The court (fixes the date; may enlarge it for cause); plaintiff (publishes and mails)

::q{#rf4}

### Supp. Rule F(6)

- **Period:** 30 days, or the time the court allows
- **Clock starts:** The date set in the notice for filing claims
- **Applies to:** Plaintiff's mailing of information to claimants
- **Binds:** Plaintiff (vessel owner)

::q{#rf6}

### Supp. Rule G(4)(a)(iii)

- **Period:** Once a week for 3 consecutive weeks (or once, if there was earlier nonjudicial notice for 30 consecutive days online or 3 weeks in a newspaper)
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Frequency of published notice in a forfeiture action
- **Binds:** Government

::q{#rg4a3}

### Supp. Rule G(4)(a)(iv)(C)

- **Period:** At least 30 consecutive days
- **Clock starts:** —
- **Applies to:** Posting notice on an official internet government forfeiture site
- **Binds:** Government

::q{#rg4a4c}

### Supp. Rule G(4)(b)(ii)(B)–(C)

- **Period:** Claim deadline at least 35 days after the notice is sent; answer or Rule 12 motion no later than 21 days after the claim
- **Clock starts:** The notice is sent; the claim is filed
- **Applies to:** Contents of direct notice to known potential claimants
- **Binds:** Government (must state these)

::q{#rg4b2}

### Supp. Rule G(5)(a)(ii)

- **Period:** The direct-notice date; or 30 days after final newspaper publication or 60 days after the first day of internet publication; or, with no notice, 60 days after filing or after § 985(c) compliance or execution of process
- **Clock starts:** See period
- **Applies to:** Filing a claim in a forfeiture action (unless the court sets a different time for good cause)
- **Binds:** Claimant

::q{#rg5a2}

### Supp. Rule G(5)(b)

- **Period:** 21 days
- **Clock starts:** Filing the claim
- **Applies to:** Answer or Rule 12 motion
- **Binds:** Claimant

### 9 U.S.C. § 4

- **Period:** 5 days' notice in writing
- **Clock starts:** Before the application is heard (counted back from the hearing; inference)
- **Applies to:** Petition for an order compelling arbitration
- **Binds:** Petitioner (serves the party in default)

### 9 U.S.C. § 4

- **Period:** On or before the return day of the notice
- **Clock starts:** The notice of application
- **Applies to:** Jury demand on whether an arbitration agreement was made or breached (not in admiralty cases)

### 9 U.S.C. § 9

- **Period:** Within 1 year
- **Clock starts:** The award is made
- **Applies to:** Application to confirm an award, where the agreement provides for judgment on the award
- **Binds:** Any party to the arbitration

### 9 U.S.C. § 12

- **Period:** Within 3 months
- **Clock starts:** The award is filed or delivered
- **Applies to:** Serving notice of a motion to vacate, modify, or correct an award
- **Binds:** The party challenging the award

::q{#t9s12}

### 9 U.S.C. § 207

- **Period:** Within 3 years
- **Clock starts:** The award is made
- **Applies to:** Application to confirm an award under the New York Convention
- **Binds:** Any party to the arbitration

::q{#t9s207}

### 9 U.S.C. § 302

- **Period:** Within 3 years (by incorporating § 207)
- **Clock starts:** The award is made
- **Applies to:** Confirming an award under the Inter-American (Panama) Convention
- **Binds:** Any party to the arbitration
