# Chronology: PO-BMS-26-0112 (Harrowgate Fastener Supply Co. / Bellwether Marine Systems, Inc.)

Prepared 2026-11-20 for the partner meeting. Covers the period from the supply agreement through Bellwether's response to the demand, plus later events the documents record (carrier claim decision, deposition).

**How to read this.** Each entry gives the date, what happened, the words people used at the time (quoted exactly), and the source file name. Text in *italics headed "Note"* is my inference, not something a document says. Three sources are scans with no machine-readable text; I read them from images, and those entries are marked **[scan, my reading]**. Where documents disagree, the entry says so, and the main conflicts are collected in the section [Where the documents disagree](#where-the-documents-disagree).

**The people.**
- *Harrowgate (seller, Columbus OH):* Dana Okafor (Account Manager) and Raymond Voss (Controller). Outside counsel: Evelyn Stroud, Pellham & Stroud LLP.
- *Bellwether (buyer, Norfolk VA):* Zoë Müller (Procurement Manager), Thomas Kettering (Receiving Supervisor), José Ibáñez (Dock 4 associate; acting lead on the afternoon of 2/24), and Marcus Delacroix-Hale (General Counsel).
- *Tri-County Freight Lines (carrier, "TCFL"):* Dispatch; D. Pruitt (delivery driver); R. Ames (pickup driver); Gary Lindqvist (Claims Manager).

**Key numbers.** PO-BMS-26-0112 · invoice INV-2026/0347, $48,612.50 · BOL TCF-889104 · PRO 0047-2219-6 · truck 4471 · discrepancy report RDR-0224-02 · receiving log entry RCV-26-0224-07 · pickup order PU-0304-118 · carrier claim TC-26-01953.

## Background: the contract terms both sides later rely on

**Nov 3, 2025: Master Supply Agreement signed.** Voss signed for Harrowgate; Delacroix-Hale signed for Bellwether.
- §5: "All deliveries are FOB Destination, Buyer's receiving dock. Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer upon delivery. 'Delivery' means physical tender at Buyer's receiving dock during receiving hours, evidenced by a delivery receipt signed by Buyer's receiving personnel."
- §6: "Each pallet must bear the Buyer purchase order number and a packing list. Buyer may quarantine or refuse any pallet that does not."
- §7.2: "Any claim of non-receipt or shortage must be made in writing within ten (10) days after the invoice date, or it is waived."
- §4.2–4.3: Net 15; late payments bear interest at 1.5% per month. §12: AAA arbitration seated in Columbus.
- Source: `master-supply-agreement-2025-11-03.docx`

## The order and shipment

**Feb 9, 2026 (Mon), 08:41: Bellwether issues PO-BMS-26-0112.** The order is for 12,000 × PN 44-B/7 bolts, 20,000 × PN 44-C/2 washers, 20,000 × PN 17-K.3/A lock nuts and 120 × PN 90-T/12 sealant, totalling $48,612.50 with estimated freight. The required-by date is Feb 27.
- The PO says: "Every pallet must carry the PO number PO-BMS-26-0112 on a packing list in a sleeve on the outside of the wrap. Pallets without a PO reference are quarantined in bay Q-3 and may be refused. The receiving supervisor or a designated dock lead must sign the carrier's delivery receipt and print their name." Its standard terms add that title and risk pass "only upon delivery to and acceptance at the Buyer receiving dock."
- Müller to Okafor: "We need it on the dock by 27 Feb — hull line 2 starts the new deck fittings the following week. Please make sure the PO number is on the packing list; our receiving team has been strict about that since January."
- Sources: `PO-BMS-26-0112.pdf` (two identical copies, Müller custodian), `2026-02-09_PO-BMS-26-0112.eml`

**Feb 9, 11:02: Harrowgate acknowledges the PO.** Okafor: "PO-BMS-26-0112 is acknowledged. … We'll ship on or about 2/19 via Tri-County, which puts it on your Dock 4 around 2/24. I've flagged the 'PO on packing list' requirement with the warehouse."
- Source: `2026-02-09_RE_PO-BMS-26-0112.eml`

**Feb 12: An earlier Harrowgate order arrives normally (for comparison).** PO-BMS-26-0098 came by "Vendor truck" to Dock 4 and was logged RECEIVED by T. Kettering. Kettering later testified: "Harrowgate's own truck brings most of their freight. I didn't connect a Tri-County pallet with no paperwork to them."
- Sources: `dock-receiving-log-2026-02.csv` (RCV-26-0212-05), `deposition-kettering-2026-04-28.pdf`

**Feb 19 (Thu): Harrowgate ships the goods.** Harrowgate's portal emailed Müller, copying Okafor: "your order PO-BMS-26-0112 left our Columbus warehouse today." The carrier was Tri-County Freight Lines; BOL TCF-889104; PRO 0047-2219-6; "1 (1,410 lb)"; estimated delivery Tuesday, Feb 24.
- Sources: `2026-02-19_shipped-PO-BMS-26-0112.eml`, `outbound-shipping-log-2026-02.csv`

**Feb 20: Harrowgate issues invoice INV-2026/0347** for $48,612.50, "Net 15 (due March 7, 2026)". The invoice describes the shipment as "1 pallet, 48″ × 40″ × 52″, 1,410 lb, shrink-wrapped, HFS label on two sides."
- Source: `INV-2026-0347.pdf` (three identical copies: one Müller, two Voss)
- *Note: under MSA §7.2, the 10-day window for a written non-receipt claim ran from this date, so it closed about March 2. The demand letter says it closed "by March 2, 2026."*

## The demand and the response

**Mar 25 (Wed): Pellham & Stroud sends a demand letter** to Delacroix-Hale by email (15:32) and certified mail, copying Voss.
- "The goods … were tendered by Tri-County Freight Lines and delivered to Bellwether's Dock 4 on February 24, 2026, as shown by the signed delivery receipt for bill of lading TCF-889104. Bellwether's assertion that 'the shipment was never received' is contradicted by that receipt and by the carrier's electronic delivery scan."
- "Section 7.2 of the Agreement required Bellwether to give written notice of any non-receipt or shortage within ten (10) days after the invoice date, i.e., by March 2, 2026. Harrowgate received no such notice. Bellwether's first communication disputing delivery was sent on March 9, 2026."
- "We understand, moreover, that on or about March 4, 2026 Bellwether released a Harrowgate pallet to the carrier as 'unclaimed.' Goods in Bellwether's possession for eight days were plainly received; Bellwether cannot avoid payment by handing them back to a third party without notice to Harrowgate."
- It demands $48,612.50 plus 1.5% monthly interest within ten days, or Harrowgate will commence AAA arbitration. It also asks Bellwether to preserve "receiving logs, dock camera footage, and communications with Tri-County Freight Lines."
- Sources: `demand-letter-2026-03-25.pdf` (three identical copies: one Delacroix-Hale, two Voss), `2026-03-25_demand-letter-Bellwether.eml`

## Where the documents disagree

1. **Was Kettering at the dock on the afternoon of Feb 24?** The roster ("PM: forklift recert, offsite"), Ibáñez's report written the same day ("Tom offsite (training)"), Kettering's camera notes ("at the forklift recert in Chesapeake from 12:30 to 17:00") and his deposition all say no. But the February receiving log has **RCV-26-0224-03, 2/24 14:52, Dock 2, UPS Freight, PO-BMS-26-0150, received_by "T. Kettering"**, the same minute as the Harrowgate pallet. Harrowgate will likely use this, and it needs an explanation (for example, a data-entry default or a later fill-in). A smaller point: a Facilities email of 3/3 announces "The annual forklift recertification for Dock 4 operators is Thursday 3/5, 06:30-08:00, in the training room" (`Dock 4 forklift recertification - Thursday 3_5.eml`). *That may be a different course from the offsite one on 2/24, but someone should ask.* The Tidewater sign-in sheet Kettering mentions is not in the collection.

2. **Did the pallet carry a packing list and PO?** Bellwether's documents all say no: the 2/24 log entry, RDR-0224-02, the 3/2 email, the pickup order ("1 pallet, unlabeled") and the Apr 2 letter. Okafor (Apr 3) says: "the packing list sleeve was on the pallet when it left Columbus. Not sure what happened to it." The carrier's receipt does carry "Shipper ref PO-BMS-26-0112 / INV-2026/0347", and the driver's handheld gave the PRO number that Ibáñez logged. *So the carrier's paperwork tied the pallet to the PO even if the pallet itself didn't.* The camera notes say a label was "visible but not readable."

3. **Was the shipment delivered? Harrowgate's own positions differ.** Harrowgate's portal, invoice, Mar 16 memo and Mar 25 demand all say it was delivered on 2/24. But on Mar 20 Harrowgate filed a claim with Tri-County **"for non-delivery"** (from the carrier's Apr 10 letter, which I read from the scan; the claim itself is not in the set). Bellwether's position has been consistent throughout: a pallet arrived, but it was never "received against" the PO.

4. **What Müller said on the Mar 2 call.** Okafor's notes say she "Did NOT say it was missing - just asking for ETA." Müller says "I'm sure I said it wasn't here," and her Mar 9 email says "I told you as much when I called on the 2nd." Bellwether's Apr 2 letter relies on the call as notice. No written notice exists before Mar 9. *A related point:* Okafor's notes have Müller saying "she'd check with receiving," yet Kettering testified that procurement first asked him on Mar 10. Müller's Mar 9 email says "Our receiving team has no record," so it is unclear whom in receiving, if anyone, she asked before Mar 10.

5. **The "TK" initials.** Harrowgate (Mar 12 email, Mar 16 memo, demand letter) and the carrier treat the receipt as signed. Bellwether's internal records (log, RDR, camera notes, deposition) show that Ibáñez, the posted acting lead, wrote "TK" on the driver's handheld. Bellwether's Apr 2 letter says only that Kettering "did not sign it" and does not name Ibáñez. The roster and the PO both require the acting lead to print their full name, which he did not do.

6. **Who held the pallet, and for how long?** The demand letter says "Goods in Bellwether's possession for eight days were plainly received." Kettering agreed it was "Eight days" (2/24–3/4). Bellwether's position is that quarantine under MSA §6 is not receipt.

7. **The PO number appears on an unrelated delivery.** The February receiving log has **RCV-26-0209-01 (2/9, Dock 2, UPS Freight, PO "PO-BMS-26-0112", vendor Norfolk Rope & Rigging, 2 pallets, RECEIVED by K. Osei)**. *This looks like a keying error, since the PO was issued only that morning to Harrowgate.* It is still a log entry showing "PO-BMS-26-0112 … RECEIVED", and it sits uneasily with Kettering's "no receipt for PO-BMS-26-0112" and his testimony.

8. **Minor discrepancies, probably not significant.**
   - The times differ by a few minutes: the camera shows the signature at 14:46:40, the receipt and scan say 14:47, and the log and RDR say 14:52 (the camera shows the move into Q-3 at 14:52:10).
   - The delivery driver (D. Pruitt) and the pickup driver (R. Ames) were different people using the same truck, 4471. That is consistent, not a conflict.
   - On the production line: Müller wrote on Mar 9 that the line "has been waiting on the 44-B/7 bolts since last week." Okafor's note of Mar 2 says the line "needs the 44-B/7 bolts by 3/9." Müller's later notes say "Line is down 44-B/7 since 9 Mar."
