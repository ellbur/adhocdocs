# Test corpus sources

The test corpus in `corpus/`: 51 documents, one directory per custodian, about
5.4 MB in total. `public/` holds real US federal government documents, kept
byte-for-byte as downloaded on 2026-10-30. The other five directories are a
synthetic, fictional commercial dispute. Paths below are relative to `corpus/`,
except the commands, which run from the top of the repository.

## The synthetic story

Harrowgate Fastener Supply Co. (seller, Columbus OH) says it delivered one pallet
under PO-BMS-26-0112 and invoice INV-2026/0347 ($48,612.50) to Bellwether Marine
Systems, Inc. (buyer, Norfolk VA) at Dock 4 on 2026-02-24. The carrier was
Tri-County Freight Lines, BOL TCF-889104, PRO 0047-2219-6. Bellwether says "the
shipment was never received". Its dock associate José Ibáñez quarantined an
unlabeled pallet with "HFS" printed on the wrap in bay Q-3, signed the carrier's
handheld as "TK", and the pallet went back to the carrier on 2026-03-04.

All people, companies, addresses, phone numbers (555-01xx) and domains
(`*.example`, IPs in 192.0.2.0/24) are fictional or reserved. The synthetic
documents are original works made for this corpus, with no third-party content.

## Regenerating

```
uv run --project /home/owen/adhocdocs python tools/corpus/build_synthetic.py   # synthetic docs (byte-reproducible)
tools/corpus/fetch_public.sh                                                   # re-download public/ (sources may change)
```

The generators sit beside this file: `build_synthetic.py` (story, most formats),
`deposition.py` (transcript), and `common.py` (PDF, scan, docx and eml helpers).
They need the Liberation fonts from `/usr/share/fonts/liberation`, or set
`ADHOC_FONT_DIR`. Scans are rendered with PyMuPDF, given seeded numpy noise,
skew and speckle, and saved as JPEG-only PDFs.

## Known facts for tests

Matches are case-insensitive, over the extracted text: the PDF text layer,
docx body and tables, the visible text of HTML, and email headers plus the
primary body part (HTML converted to text).

1. **Phrase `"the shipment was never received"`** matches 10 files:
   - `muller-z/2026-03-09_RE_INV-2026-0347_past-due.eml` and `okafor-d/2026-03-09_RE_INV-2026-0347_past-due.eml` (original text)
   - `okafor-d/2026-03-12_proof-of-delivery.eml` (in quoted text only)
   - `muller-z/2026-03-17_RE_proof-of-delivery.eml` (new text and quoted text)
   - `kettering-t/2026-03-10_RE_dock-4-check-PO-BMS-26-0112.eml`
   - `muller-z/dispute-timeline-notes.txt`
   - `okafor-d/call-notes-bellwether-2026-03.txt`
   - `voss-r/memo-collection-bellwether-2026-03-16.pdf` and `voss-r/memo-collection-bellwether-2026-03-18-rev.pdf`
   - `delacroix-m/deposition-kettering-2026-04-28.pdf`, at **6:12–6:13**, across a line break ("…the shipment was" / "never received.")

   It also occurs, split across a line break ("…“the shipment" / "was never
   received”…"), in `voss-r/demand-letter-2026-03-25.pdf` and in its two
   attachment copies. A search that joins lines should find it there.

   The shorter **`"never received"`** matches those 10 plus 6 more:
   `voss-r/demand-letter-2026-03-25.pdf`, `voss-r/ar-aging-2026-03-31.csv`,
   `muller-z/response-to-demand-2026-04-02.docx`,
